Projects

Saudi Market Sales Intelligence Dashboard

Overview

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing the performance of "Saudi Market", a multi-branch supermarket chain operating across 12 branches in Greater Cairo (Nasr City, City Stars, Maadi, Heliopolis, Zamalek, Dokki, New Cairo, Mohandeseen, Sheikh Zayed, 6th of October, and more) over a full year.

What I Built

  • KPI Cards – six headline performance indicators at a glance.
  • Sales Trend Over Time – monthly sales line chart to spot seasonality.
  • Sales by Branch & Profit by Branch – side-by-side branch performance comparison.
  • Sales by Category – donut chart showing revenue share across Grocery, Nuts, Oils, Coffee, Spices, Household, Beverages, and more.
  • Top 10 Products by Sales – best-selling products ranking.
  • Sales by Customer Type – new vs. returning customer revenue.
  • Sales by Channel – in-store vs. online comparison.
  • Interactive Slicers – Branch, Month, Category, and Channel filters for dynamic, drill-down analysis.

Key Insights & Outcomes

  • Strong Profitability: A 41.67% profit margin indicates healthy retail profitability.
  • Branch Performance: Nasr City and City Stars lead with approximately 95K in sales, while 6th of October and Mall of Arabia trail at around 65K, highlighting opportunities for improvement.
  • Category Mix: Grocery accounts for the largest revenue share (21.2%), followed by Nuts (17.6%) and Oils (12.77%).
  • Top-Selling Product: Olive Oil 500ml leads with approximately 70K in sales, followed by Sunflower Oil 1L and Rice 5kg.
  • Customer Loyalty: Returning customers generate nearly twice the sales of new customers, indicating strong retention alongside an opportunity to attract new customers.
  • Sales Channel Gap: In-store sales (approximately 0.8M) significantly exceed online sales (approximately 0.2M), highlighting an opportunity to grow e-commerce.
  • Seasonality: Sales peak in October and November at approximately 90K, with noticeable declines in September and December.

Superstore Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

Overview

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing the sales performance of a US-based retail company (Superstore) across four regions (West, East, South, and Central) from 2016 to 2019.

The interactive Power BI dashboard helps management monitor sales, profitability, and customer behavior, identify business trends, and uncover opportunities to improve financial performance.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Total Sales: 2.30M
  • Total Profit: 286.40K
  • Profit Margin: 12.47%
  • Order Count: 5K
  • Customer Count: 794

What I Built

  1. KPI Cards: Five headline performance indicators for monitoring business performance.
  2. Sales Trend Over Time: Yearly sales line chart showing growth from 2016 to 2019.
  3. Sales by Category: Donut chart comparing revenue shares across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
  4. Profit by Region: Bar chart comparing profitability across the four regions.
  5. Sales by Customer Segment: Revenue comparison across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
  6. Interactive Slicers: Region, Category, Ship Mode, Year, and Segment filters for dynamic analysis.

Key Insights & Outcomes

  1. Consistent Growth: Sales increased from approximately 484K in 2016 to 733K in 2019, with the strongest acceleration between 2017 and 2018.
  2. Category Performance: Technology leads with 36.4% of sales (836K), followed by Furniture (32.3%) and Office Supplies (31.3%).
  3. Regional Profit Gap: West generates the highest profit at approximately 108K, followed by East at 92K, while South (47K) and Central (40K) lag behind.
  4. Customer Segment Performance: Consumer customers generate approximately 1.16M in sales, ahead of Corporate (0.71M) and Home Office (0.43M).
  5. Profitability Opportunity: The 12.47% profit margin highlights an opportunity to review discounting, product costs, and other factors affecting profitability.

Technologies

Power BI Desktop DAX Power Query (M) Excel / CSV Star Schema

FIFA World Cup 2026 Analytics Dashboard

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the FIFA World Cup 2026, covering 48 teams, 104 matches, and the three host countries: the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The dashboard provides insights into match distribution, results, goals, and tournament stages.

Key Performance Indicators

  • Total Matches: 104
  • Total Goals: 308
  • Participating Teams: 48
  • Home Wins: 51
  • Draws: 24
  • Away Wins: 29
  • Average Goals per Match: 2.96

What I Built

  • Interactive KPI cards summarizing tournament statistics.
  • Matches distribution by tournament stage.
  • Matches hosted by each country.
  • Daily match distribution using an area chart.
  • Interactive slicers for Group, Stage, Confederation, and Country.

Data Analysis & Modeling

  • Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query.
  • Designed a data model with a fact table and dimensions for teams, stages, venues, countries, and dates.
  • Created DAX measures to calculate tournament KPIs and match statistics.
  • Designed an interactive dashboard to simplify tournament analysis.

Key Insights

  • The Group Stage accounts for approximately 72 matches, representing around 69% of the tournament.
  • The Round of 32 includes 16 matches, while the Round of 16 includes 8 matches.
  • Match results include 51 home wins, 29 away wins, and 24 draws.
  • The United States hosts approximately 78 matches, while Canada and Mexico host around 13 matches each.
  • The schedule reaches a peak of 6 matches per day, with quieter days featuring only one match.
